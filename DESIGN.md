## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a refined, state-of-the-art medical and physical rehabilitation platform. The visual tone blends clinical precision with human empathy: clean, modern, reliable, and fundamentally optimistic. It strips away the clinical dread and sterile severity common to legacy healthcare platforms, replacing them with breathable negative space, precise typography, and soft, kinetic color harmonies.

### Design Movement
**Minimalist Human-Centered Modernism** infused with subtle soft-depth dimension:
- **Calm & Measured Whitespace:** Generous internal padding and spatial rhythm to reduce cognitive fatigue for both clinicians tracking caseloads and patients navigating recovery protocols.
- **Architectural Crispness:** Softened clinical borders, refined geometry, and light, airy container cards that communicate absolute structure, hygiene, and competence.
- **Supportive Vitality:** Micro-moments of kinetic energy using gentle teal and restorative purple accents for recovery milestones, range-of-motion metrics, and positive progress affirmations.

## Colors

The color system delivers high legibility and calming assurance, supporting clinical accuracy and accessibility compliance (WCAG AAA for typography, AA for interactive UI).

- **Primary (`#1E40AF` — Clinical Cobalt):** Deep, confident medical blue representing surgical authority, diagnostics, institutional trust, and structural focus. Used for primary calls-to-action, key navigational states, and clinical anchors.
- **Secondary (`#0D9488` — Restorative Teal):** Fresh, therapeutic aqua representing healing, biological recovery, range of motion, and successful rehabilitation milestones.
- **Tertiary (`#7C3AED` — Neuro Violet):** Intelligent, soft purple used sparingly for smart analytics, AI-assisted movement diagnostics, patient compliance streaks, and specialized therapy categories.
- **Neutral Core (`#0F172A` / `#1E293B` — Deep Slate Navy):** Replaces harsh pure black with rich, high-contrast slate. Anchors body copy, charts, and technical text with high clarity.
- **Surfaces & Canvas:** Canvas base utilizes `#F8FAFC` (Cool Off-White), layered with elevated pure white (`#FFFFFF`) cards and structural separator borders in `#E2E8F0` (Subtle Cool Slate).

## Typography

Typography relies uniformly on **Plus Jakarta Sans**, chosen for its friendly geometric geometry, open apertures, and exceptional legibility across clinical dashboards and patient mobile interfaces. 

- **Headlines:** Display weights leverage `-0.02em` tracking and medium-to-bold weights to convey dependable authority without feeling rigid.
- **Body Text:** Standard body scales maintain generous line-heights (150% to 160%) to prevent reading fatigue during lengthy diagnostic review and therapy regime reviews.
- **Data & Numeric Readouts:** Angular degrees (e.g., flexion, extension), repetition counts, and heart rates utilize `tabular-nums` formatting to ensure absolute alignment in patient observation tables.

## Layout & Spacing

The platform is structured on an **8-point spatial cadence** aligned with a fluid, 12-column responsive layout grid.

- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and generous 32px (`2rem`) page margins. Maximum content width capped at 1440px for comfortable panoramic review.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 24px margins. Clinical multi-column schedules reflow to tabbed modular cards.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and outer margins. High-density data dashboards reflow to vertically stacked cards with edge-to-edge scrollable metrics chips.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is established using **ambient, diffused drop shadows** combined with subtle border delineation to avoid muddy overlays and preserve a crisp, clinical aesthetic.

- **Surface Level 0 (Canvas):** `#F8FAFC`. The foundational backdrop for all page views.
- **Surface Level 1 (Default Containers & Cards):** `#FFFFFF` framed with an ultra-fine border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`). Shadow: `0px 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0px 4px 8px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Surface Level 2 (Interactive Hover / Modals / Active Patient Tiles):** `#FFFFFF` with heightened diffuse suspension: `0px 6px 16px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06), 0px 3px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Drawers / Popovers / Floating Video Assessment Tools):** `#FFFFFF` with dramatic ambient blur: `0px 16px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0px 6px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **roundedness level of 2** (`0.5rem` / `8px` base, scaling to `12px` - `16px` for cards and content groups). 

- Standard inputs, buttons, and badges utilize `8px` to `10px` radii to preserve structured reliability.
- Patient overview cards, clinical diagnostic containers, and exercise cards adopt `12px` to `16px` (`rounded-lg` / `rounded-xl`) radii, establishing a comfortable, modern consumer-health quality.
- Progress pills, active status pills, and biometric metric badges use continuous full-pill rounding (`rounded-full`) for quick visual indexing.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#1E40AF` fill with `#FFFFFF` text. Border radius of `10px`, padding `10px 20px`. Subtle hover elevation (`#1D4ED8`) with gentle active scale down (`0.98`).
- **Secondary:** Surface `#FFFFFF` with `#E2E8F0` border and `#1E40AF` text. Hover brings `#F1F5F9` background and `#CBD5E1` border.
- **Tertiary (Therapy/Insight Actions):** Soft `#F5F3FF` lavender fill with `#7C3AED` text for specialized rehab actions and AI biomechanical feedback.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background: `#FFFFFF`. Border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`, rounded to `8px`.
- Active / Focus: Border changes to `#1E40AF` with an outer halo ring `0 0 0 3px rgba(30, 64, 175, 0.12)`.
- Label typography: `label-md` in `#475569`, aligned 6px above field boundary.

### Cards & Patient Data Blocks
- Encased in `#FFFFFF` with a 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `14px` corner radii, and Level 1 elevation.
- Dividers within cards utilize `#F1F5F9` hairpins with 16px internal padding.

### Status Chips & Badges
- **Active / Recovering:** Subtle teal background (`#F0FDFA`) with `#0D9488` text and an optional `#14B8A6` glowing status dot.
- **Scheduled / Pending:** Cool slate background (`#F1F5F9`) with `#475569` text.
- **Caution / Flare-Up:** Soft rose background (`#FFF1F2`) with `#E11D48` text.

### Range of Motion & Biometric Gauges
- Specialized components representing joint angle tracking (degrees) and muscle activation use clean `#0D9488` stroke tracks against a muted `#E2E8F0` path, accented by sharp, geometric numerical callouts.
